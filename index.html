MOOD 51. FOOTBALL, RECREATION AND MASCULINE SOCIALIZATION⚽🥅🥅⚽

This month, Social Mood Label explores the relationship between football, recreation, and masculine socialization.

As the FIFA World Cup captures global attention, this discussion moves beyond the game itself to examine how football spaces shape identity, belonging, behaviour, and ideas of manhood.

From boys playing in eKasi streets and community grounds to men gathering for recreational football after work and on weekends, football remains one of the most influential social spaces in the lives of many South African males.

Yet these spaces also exist within a context marked by inequality, violence, and unequal access to recreation.

We must ask ourselves, what kinds of values, norms, and masculinities are being cultivated through football, and what role can recreation play in building healthier communities?.
